## What We Do

USA Concrete Jobs connects homeowners and businesses with licensed, insured concrete contractors across 5 states. We specialize in:

- **Concrete Cutting** — Flat sawing, wall sawing, wire sawing, core drilling
- **Waterproofing** — Basement waterproofing, foundation waterproofing, exterior waterproofing
- **Concrete Finishing** — Stamped concrete, polished concrete, stained concrete, sealed concrete
- **Core Drilling** — Precision core drilling for plumbing, electrical, HVAC penetrations
- **Driveway Replacement** — Concrete driveway removal and replacement
- **Foundation Repair** — Crack repair, structural foundation repair, piering
